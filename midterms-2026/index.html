How to read the ratings

Solid: not expected to be competitive. Likely: competitive but one party has a clear edge. Lean: competitive, one party has a small edge. Toss Up: either side can win.

A tile with an inner ring holds a seat currently favored to change parties.

The new House maps

Several states redrew districts mid-decade. Texas added roughly five GOP-leaning seats; California's Proposition 50 answered with roughly five Democratic-leaning ones. Ohio, Missouri, North Carolina, Florida and Utah also have new lines. Missouri's map is on the ballot as a referendum this year.

Election night timing

Most of Kentucky closes at 6pm ET, Georgia and Virginia at 7, North Carolina and Ohio at 7:30, and Maine, Michigan, New Hampshire and Pennsylvania at 8. Most of Texas, Iowa and Kansas close at 9pm ET. Alaska closes last, and its ranked-choice count can take days.
